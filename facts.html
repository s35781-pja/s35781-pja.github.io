Interesting Facts About Food

🍓 Strawberries are the only fruit with seeds on the outside.

🍫 Chocolate was once used as money by the ancient Maya civilization.

🍯 Honey never spoils and can last for thousands of years.

🥕 Carrots were originally purple, not orange.

🍎 Apples float in water because they are about 25% air.

🍌 Bananas are berries, but strawberries are not.

🍕 The world's largest pizza was over 1,200 square meters in size.

🥞 Pancakes have been eaten for thousands of years and are one of the oldest breakfast foods.

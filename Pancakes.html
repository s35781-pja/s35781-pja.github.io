Pancakes
🕓 Prep Time: 10 mins 🍳 Cook Time: 25 mins 💛 Medium
Ingredients
  • 1 cup flour
  • 1 cup milk
  • 1 egg
Directions
  1. Mix all ingredients together.
  2. Pour batter onto a hot griddle.
  3. Cook until golden brown on both sides.
🔥 250 kcal 🍬 12g sugar 💪 8g protein

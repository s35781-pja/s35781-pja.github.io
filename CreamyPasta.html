Creamy Pasta
🕓 Prep Time: 15 mins 🍳 Cook Time: 12 mins
❤️ Easy
Ingredients
  • 200g pasta
  • 100ml cream sauce
  • Salt and pepper to taste
Directions
  1. Boil pasta according to package instructions.
  2. Drain pasta and return to pot.
  3. Add cream sauce to pasta and stir until well combined.
  4. Season with salt and pepper to taste.
  5. Serve hot and enjoy!
🔥 550 kcal 🍭 5g sugar 💪 18g protein

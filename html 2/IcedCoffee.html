Iced Coffee
🕓 Prep Time: 7 mins 🍳 Cook Time: 5 mins
❤️ Easy
Ingredients
  • 1 cup cold brew coffee
  • 1 cup milk
  • 2 tablespoons sugar
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
  • 1 cup ice cubes
Directions
  1. In a glass, combine cold brew coffee, milk, sugar, and vanilla extract. Stir until the sugar is dissolved.
  2. Add ice cubes to the glass and stir again.
  3. Enjoy your refreshing iced coffee!

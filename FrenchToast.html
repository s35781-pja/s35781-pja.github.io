French Toast
🕓 Prep Time: 5 mins 🍳 Cook Time: 10 mins
💛 Medium
Ingredients
  • 4 slices bread
  • 2 large eggs
  • 1 tbsp unsalted butter
Directions
  1. Whisk milk, eggs, vanilla, cinnamon, and salt together in a bowl.
  2. Lightly butter a griddle or skillet and heat over medium-high heat.
  3. Soak bread in the egg mixture, soaking both sides. Transfer to the hot skillet and cook until golden, 3 to 4 minutes per side.
🔥 320 kcal 🍭 15g sugar 💪 10g protein
